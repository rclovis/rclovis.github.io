Privacy Policy

Knock Lane

Last updated: 18 November 2026

English Français

This game collects nothing. It has no server, no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not even ask Android for permission to use the internet. Your best distance and your sound setting stay on your device.

1. Who is responsible

Knock Lane (package com.rclovis.knocklane) is developed and published by an individual developer publishing as rclovis, acting as the data controller for the purposes of this policy.

Contact: lipidgames.panama090@aleeas.com

2. What the game stores on your device

Two things, written to the game's private storage on your device:

  • your best distance,
  • whether the sound is on or off.

Neither leaves your phone. The game cannot send them anywhere, because it has no access to the network. Both are deleted when you uninstall the game, or when you clear its storage from Android's app settings.

3. What the game collects

Nothing. There is no advertising, no in-app purchase, no sign-in and no third-party code that collects data. The game uses one Android permission, vibration, to make the phone buzz when a ball hits.

4. Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. When you install or update it, Google processes data under its own privacy policy. The developer receives only aggregate statistics from Google, such as the number of installs per country, never anything that identifies you.

5. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. Since it collects no data at all, it collects none from them either.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and comparable laws you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. The developer holds no personal data about you, but you are welcome to write to the contact address above and you will get an answer.

7. Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts collecting anything, this policy will change first: the new version will be published at this same address, with the date at the top updated, and the change noted in the game's release notes on Google Play.
